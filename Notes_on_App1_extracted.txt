1. Digit Span = CapacityCan the mind hold a thread?

2. Stroop = ControlCan the mind resist the wrong impulse?
Show color words in mismatched colors.

Score: time + errors. 
3. Short CPT = EnduranceCan the mind stay alert without drifting?
Flash letters/symbols. 
User taps only target item. 
Score: misses + false taps + reaction time.
